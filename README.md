## Featured Project

### Debbylytics Deluxe Store: Sales Performance Dashboard
An individual capstone project analysing the sales performance of a retail store, with all values in Naira (₦).

**What I did**
- Cleaned 9,994 records down to 9,993 by removing one exact duplicate, keeping repeated Order IDs and storing Postal Code as text
- Built a data model with one fact table and six dimension tables in Excel and Power Query
- Designed a 4-page Power BI dashboard:
  1. Sales Overview
  2. Product and Category Profitability
  3. Regional and Sales Team Performance
  4. Discount and Shipping Analysis
- Prepared a presentation with business insights and recommendations


### Headline numbers
| Revenue | Profit | Orders | Profit Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₦2,297,200.86 | ₦286,397.02 | 5,009 | 12% |

### key insights
1. **Sales and profit peak toward the end of the year.** Revenue climbs from September and peaks in November, and profit is highest in December. February and July are the weakest months.
   *My recommendation:* prepare stock and marketing before September, and use promotions to lift the slow months.

2. **Furniture is the weak spot.** Technology (₦836,154) and Office Supplies (₦719,047) earn a 17% margin, but Furniture earns only 2% on ₦742,000 in revenue.
   *My recommendation:* review Furniture pricing and costs, and reduce discounts on it.

3. **One product drives a large share of profit.** The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier earns ₦61,600 in revenue and ₦25,200 in profit, far ahead of every other product.
   *My recommendation:* keep top products well stocked, and reduce dependence on a single product.

4. **West and East lead; Central and South lag.** West (₦725,458) and East (₦678,781) generate the most revenue, while South is lowest at ₦391,722.
   *My recommendation:* investigate costs and discounting in Central and South before investing in growth there.

5. **Consumers are the biggest segment**, followed by Corporate and Home Office.
   *My recommendation:* retain Consumer customers and target Corporate buyers for bulk orders.

6. **Heavy discounts destroy profit.** Orders with no discount earn the most profit, while very high discounts make a loss. The average discount is 16%.
   *My recommendation:* cap discounts at around 20% and require approval above that.

7. **Standard Class carries most of the revenue, but First Class is the most profitable shipping mode** (about 14% margin against about 12%).
   *My recommendation:* encourage faster shipping options and review Standard Class delivery costs.
